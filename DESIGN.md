# Scribe design system

Scribe feels precise, quiet, and inspectable. Use semantic surface, text, border, focus, status, and evidence tokens; avoid hard-coded brand colors in components.

The workspace progressively reveals Sources, Conversation, and Evidence. All three remain visible on wide screens, secondary regions collapse on medium screens, and mobile uses one region at a time. Evidence uses a consistent citation anatomy: reference number, file name, page or section, excerpt, and an optional retrieval score in expert mode.

Controls use concise sentence-case labels and at least 44px touch targets. Focus is visible and never color-only. Motion communicates state, respects reduced motion, and avoids decorative choreography, bounce, glow, and ambient pulsing.
