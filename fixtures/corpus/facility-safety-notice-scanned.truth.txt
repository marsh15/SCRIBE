NORTHWIND LOGISTICS - SAFETY NOTICE FSN-2026-014

Notice reference FSN-2026-014 takes effect on 2 March 2026
at depot D-RTM, Rotterdam.

High-visibility vests are mandatory in all loading areas.
Forklifts must not exceed 8 km/h inside the warehouse and
15 km/h in the yard.

The maximum rack load is 1,250 kg per pallet level. The
assembly point for evacuation is Point B at the north
fence.

A fire drill is held on the first Tuesday of every month
at 10:15.

In case of a life-threatening situation on any Northwind
site, call the site emergency line 0800-555-0112 first and
the Rotterdam control room on +31 10 555 0198 second.

Questions about this notice go to the depot manager, Dirk
van Leeuwen, on extension 4417.
