Postmortem: GPS feed outages, November 2025
===========================================

Incident number: INC-2025-117. Severity: SEV2. Author: Priya Raman, CTO.

Summary
-------

On 18 November 2025 the Helios Telematics GPS feed was unavailable for 221
minutes starting at 08:12 UTC. A second outage on 27 November 2025 lasted 77
minutes. The total downtime in November 2025 was 298 minutes, giving monthly
availability of 99.31%.

Impact
------

The outage affected 412 vehicles and delayed 1,930 shipments. No customer data
was lost.

Detection
---------

Monitoring detected the first outage after 19 minutes. Nobody was alerted
automatically because the alert threshold was set to 30 minutes.

Root cause
----------

The root cause was an expired TLS certificate on the Helios ingest gateway.
Helios had not renewed the certificate because the reminder email went to a
mailbox of a former employee.

Action items
------------

- INV-2047: make webhook delivery idempotent (owner Priya Raman, due
  2026-03-31).

- Reduce the alert threshold for GPS feed gaps from 30 minutes to 5 minutes
  (owner Lars Hendriksen).

- Ask Helios to confirm the service credit for November 2025 (owner Jonas
  Albrecht).
