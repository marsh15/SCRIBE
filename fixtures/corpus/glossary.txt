Northwind Glossary
==================

A to M
------

Cross-dock: a facility where inbound freight is transferred to outbound
vehicles with less than 24 hours of storage.

Depot code: a short identifier such as D-RTM for Rotterdam or D-HAM for
Hamburg.

Harbour: the internal HR portal used for leave, timesheets and payslips.

Hub: a depot that sorts at least 500,000 parcels per quarter.

N to Z
------

NorthExpense: the tool used to file expense claims.

POD: proof of delivery, a signature or photo captured at the consignee.

SEV1: the highest incident severity, requiring acknowledgement within 15
minutes.

SKU prefix NW-PAL means pallet, NW-BOX means carton, and NW-COLD means
cold-chain packaging.

Tier A city: one of Amsterdam, Paris, Milan, London and Zurich, where higher
hotel caps apply.
