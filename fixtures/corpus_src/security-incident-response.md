# Security Incident Response Plan

Owner: Tomasz Kowalski, CISO. ⟦Version 3.4 was approved on 20 January 2026.⟧

## 1. Scope

1.1 This plan covers any event that threatens the confidentiality, integrity or availability of Northwind systems or data, including lost devices, phishing, ransomware and unauthorised access.

1.2 ⟦The Security Operations Centre can be reached 24/7 on +31 10 555 0142.⟧

1.3 ⟦Every incident is logged as a ticket with the prefix SEC- (for example SEC-3391).⟧

<!-- pagebreak -->
## 2. Severity levels

| Severity | Definition | Acknowledge within | Resolve target |
|---|---|---|---|
⟦| SEV1 | Total outage or confirmed data breach | 15 minutes | 4 hours |⟧
⟦| SEV2 | Major degradation of a customer-facing service | 30 minutes | 8 hours |⟧
| SEV3 | Minor degradation with a workaround | 4 hours | 3 days |
| SEV4 | Informational or cosmetic | 1 business day | 10 days |

2.1 ⟦A SEV1 incident must be acknowledged within 15 minutes and escalated to the CISO, Tomasz Kowalski.⟧

2.2 ⟦Critical incidents must be reported to the Security Operations Centre within 30 minutes of detection.⟧

<!-- pagebreak -->
## 3. Roles and escalation

3.1 The incident commander is the on-call security engineer. The communications lead is Marta Veldkamp's office for SEV1 incidents and the Head of Operations for all others.

3.2 ⟦Ransom payments must never be made without written approval from both the CEO and the CISO.⟧

## 4. Data protection

4.1 ⟦Personal data breaches must be reported to the Data Protection Officer within 24 hours of discovery.⟧

4.2 ⟦Personal data breaches must be notified to the supervisory authority within 72 hours of discovery.⟧

## 5. Evidence and review

5.1 ⟦Forensic images are retained for 18 months.⟧

5.2 ⟦A post-incident review must be held within 5 working days of closure of any SEV1 or SEV2 incident.⟧

5.3 ⟦Exception: SEV1 incidents caused by a third-party outage are reviewed within 10 working days.⟧

## 6. Emergency contact

⟦In case of a life-threatening situation on any Northwind site, call the site emergency line 0800-555-0112 first and the Rotterdam control room on +31 10 555 0199 second.⟧
