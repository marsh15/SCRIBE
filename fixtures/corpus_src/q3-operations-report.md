# Q3 2026 Operations Report

Prepared by Lars Hendriksen, Head of Operations, for the Management Board. Reporting period: 1 July to 30 September 2026.

## 1. Summary

⟦Q3 2026 revenue was EUR 228.9 million, up 7.8% from Q2.⟧ ⟦The Milan hub missed its on-time target of 95% for the third consecutive quarter.⟧ ⟦Fleet availability averaged 91.6% across all depots.⟧

⟦The Lyon cross-dock expansion opened on 14 August 2026 and adds 6 loading doors.⟧ ⟦Three lost-time injuries were recorded in Q3, versus five in Q2.⟧

## 5. Safety and fleet

5.1 The lost-time injury frequency rate fell from 1.9 to 1.1 per million hours worked.

5.2 ⟦Cold-chain excursions above 8 degrees C numbered 14 in Q3, down from 22 in Q2.⟧

5.3 Fleet availability was lowest at Austin (86.0%) and highest at Rotterdam (95.1%).

## 6. Outlook

⟦The Board approved a Q4 2026 peak-season hiring plan of 180 temporary warehouse staff.⟧ The Austin depot lease review is scheduled for November 2026.
