## 2. Service levels

2.1 ⟦Helios guarantees a monthly availability of 99.9% for the GPS and telemetry feed.⟧

2.2 ⟦Scheduled maintenance is excluded from availability and is limited to Sundays 02:00 to 04:00 CET, at most 4 times per month.⟧

2.3 ⟦A priority 1 support request must receive a first response within 20 minutes, 24/7.⟧ Priority 2 requests receive a first response within 2 hours during business hours.

## 3. Service credits

If monthly availability falls below the guarantee, Northwind receives a credit as a percentage of the monthly fee.

| Monthly availability | Service credit |
|---|---|
⟦| 99.90% or higher | 0% |⟧
⟦| 99.50% to 99.89% | 5% |⟧
⟦| 99.00% to 99.49% | 10% |⟧
⟦| Below 99.00% | 25% |⟧

3.1 ⟦Service credits are capped at 30% of the monthly fee and are applied to the next invoice.⟧

3.2 ⟦Credits are not available for outages caused by Northwind's own network or by force majeure.⟧
