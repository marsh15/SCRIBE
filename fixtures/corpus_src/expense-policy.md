# Northwind Logistics Expense Policy

Document owner: Jonas Albrecht, CFO. ⟦Version 5.2 took effect on 1 March 2026 and replaces Version 5.1.⟧

## 1. General rules

1.1 ⟦Expense claims are filed in the expense tool "NorthExpense" under cost code EXP-TRV for travel or EXP-ENT for entertainment.⟧

1.2 ⟦Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date.⟧ Claims submitted between day 31 and day 90 need approval from the Finance Controller.

1.3 ⟦Claims submitted after 90 days are rejected without exception.⟧

1.4 ⟦Receipts are mandatory for any single expense above EUR 25.⟧ Lost receipts require a signed declaration approved by a Director.

<!-- pagebreak -->
## 2. Limits and approvals

2.1 ⟦Claims above EUR 1,500 require approval from a Director, and claims above EUR 10,000 require approval from the CFO.⟧

2.2 ⟦The standard corporate card limit is EUR 3,000 per month.⟧ Higher limits can be requested through the Finance Controller.

2.3 The following category limits apply:

| Category | Limit | Approver |
|---|---|---|
⟦| Client entertainment | EUR 85 per person | Director |⟧
⟦| Mileage (private car) | EUR 0.23 per km | Line manager |⟧
⟦| Taxi and ride-hailing | EUR 60 per trip | Line manager |⟧
⟦| Conference tickets | EUR 1,200 per event | Director |⟧
| Parking | EUR 25 per day | Line manager |

<!-- pagebreak -->
## 3. Travel expenses

3.1 ⟦Meals during domestic travel are reimbursed up to EUR 45 per day.⟧ Meals during international travel are reimbursed up to EUR 62 per day.

3.2 ⟦Hotel nights are capped at EUR 160 in Tier A cities and EUR 120 elsewhere.⟧ Tier A cities are Amsterdam, Paris, Milan, London and Zurich.

3.3 ⟦Alcohol is not reimbursable except at client events approved in advance by a Director.⟧

3.4 ⟦Reimbursement is paid on the 25th of each month following approval.⟧
