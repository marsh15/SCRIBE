# Master Services Agreement

## Parties and effective date

⟦This Master Services Agreement MSA-2026-0087 is made between Northwind Logistics B.V. (the Provider) and Fjordline Retail AS (the Customer) with effect from 1 April 2026.⟧

## 1. Term

### 1.1 Initial term

⟦The initial term is 36 months from the effective date.⟧ After the initial term the Agreement renews for successive periods of 12 months.

## 2. Services

### 2.1 Scope

The Provider shall transport, store and handle the Customer's goods between the Customer's sites in Norway, Sweden, Denmark and the Netherlands as described in each Order.

### 2.2 Rates

The rates below apply to all Orders. All rates are in EUR and exclude VAT.

| Service | Unit | Rate |
|---|---|---|
⟦| EU road freight, full pallet | per pallet | 38.50 |⟧
⟦| Cold-chain surcharge | per pallet | 6.75 |⟧
⟦| Pallet storage | per pallet per week | 3.20 |⟧
⟦| Fuel surcharge | percent of freight | 6.5% |⟧

## 3. Delivery

### 3.1 Delay credit

⟦The Provider shall credit 5% of the shipment charge for each full day of delay, up to a maximum of 25%.⟧

### 3.2 Delay credit exceptions

⟦No delay credit is payable where the delay is caused by customs inspection, force majeure, or incorrect data supplied by the Customer.⟧

## 4. Fees and payment

### 4.1 Invoicing

⟦Invoices are payable within 30 days of the invoice date.⟧

### 4.2 Late payment

⟦Late payments accrue interest at 1.5% per month.⟧

### 4.3 Disputed invoices

⟦Notwithstanding clause 4.2, invoices disputed in good faith within 10 days of receipt are not subject to late interest.⟧

## 5. Liability

### 5.1 General cap

⟦The Provider's total aggregate liability in any contract year shall not exceed EUR 2,000,000.⟧

### 5.2 Exclusions from the cap

⟦The liability cap in clause 5.1 does not apply to death or personal injury, fraud, or wilful misconduct.⟧

### 5.3 Confidentiality breach

⟦For breach of clause 8 (Confidentiality) the Provider's liability is capped at EUR 5,000,000 rather than the amount in clause 5.1.⟧

### 5.4 Insurance

⟦The Provider shall maintain cargo insurance of at least EUR 3,000,000 per occurrence.⟧

## 6. Data protection

Personal data is processed under the Data Processing Addendum ⟦DPA-2026-011⟧, which forms part of this Agreement. In the event of conflict on personal data, the Data Processing Addendum prevails.

## 7. Termination

### 7.1 For convenience

⟦Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience on 90 days' written notice.⟧ ⟦However, the Customer may not give such notice before 1 April 2027.⟧

### 7.2 For cause

⟦Notwithstanding clause 7.1, either party may terminate immediately if the other becomes insolvent.⟧ A material breach that remains uncured 30 days after written notice also entitles the other party to terminate.

## 8. Confidentiality

Each party shall keep the other's confidential information secret. This obligation survives termination for 5 years.

## 9. Governing law

⟦This Agreement is governed by Dutch law and the courts of Rotterdam have exclusive jurisdiction.⟧
