# Employment Agreement Template

This template is used for permanent employees in the Netherlands. Bracketed fields are completed by HR for each hire.

## 1. Parties and start date

This agreement is made between Northwind Logistics B.V., Rotterdam, and [Employee Name], starting on [Start Date], in the role of [Job Title].

## 2. Probation

### 2.1 Probation period

⟦The probation period is 6 months.⟧ During probation either party may terminate with 1 month's written notice.

## 3. Hours and place of work

### 3.1 Working hours

⟦The regular working week is 38 hours, spread over five days.⟧

### 3.2 Place of work

The place of work is the site named in the offer letter. ⟦The employer may reassign the employee to another site within 50 km of the place of work with 4 weeks' notice.⟧

## 4. Remuneration

Remuneration is set out in the individual offer letter and is not part of this template.

## 5. Leave

Annual leave is governed by the Leave Policy in force on 1 January of the relevant calendar year. The policy is available in the Harbour portal.

## 6. Notice periods

After probation the following notice periods apply to termination by the employee and by the employer.

| Length of service | Notice period |
|---|---|
⟦| Up to 5 years | 2 months |⟧
⟦| 5 to 10 years | 3 months |⟧
⟦| More than 10 years | 4 months |⟧

## 7. Confidentiality and non-compete

### 7.1 Confidentiality

⟦The employee shall keep business secrets confidential during and for 3 years after employment.⟧

### 7.2 Non-compete

⟦A non-compete applies for 6 months after termination, within the Benelux region.⟧ ⟦The non-compete applies only to employees in job grade G7 or above.⟧

## 8. Intellectual property

All inventions and software developed in the course of employment belong to Northwind Logistics B.V.

## 9. Garden leave

⟦During a notice period the employer may place the employee on garden leave for up to 30 days.⟧

## 10. Governing law

This agreement is governed by Dutch law.
