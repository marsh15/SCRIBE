# Travel Guidelines

## 1. Booking

### 1.1 Tool and lead time

⟦All business travel is booked through the NorthTravel tool at least 14 days before departure.⟧

### 1.2 Flights

⟦Economy class is mandatory for flights shorter than 4 hours.⟧ ⟦Business class is allowed on flights longer than 4 hours for Directors and above.⟧

### 1.3 Rail

⟦Rail is the preferred mode for journeys under 500 km.⟧

## 2. Accommodation and meals

### 2.1 Hotels

⟦Hotel nights in Tier A cities are capped at EUR 175.⟧ Elsewhere the cap is EUR 120 per night.

### 2.2 Meals

⟦Meals during domestic travel are reimbursed up to EUR 48 per day.⟧

### 2.3 Daily allowance table

| Trip type | Meal allowance | Incidentals |
|---|---|---|
⟦| Domestic | EUR 48 | EUR 5 |⟧
⟦| Europe | EUR 62 | EUR 8 |⟧
⟦| Outside Europe | EUR 75 | EUR 12 |⟧

## 3. Private vehicles and insurance

### 3.1 Mileage

⟦Mileage in a private car is reimbursed at EUR 0.23 per km.⟧

### 3.2 Insurance

⟦Travel insurance is automatically included for trips booked via NorthTravel.⟧

## 4. Safety

Travellers must register trips outside Europe with the Security Operations Centre at least 5 working days before departure.
