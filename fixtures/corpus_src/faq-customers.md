# Customer FAQ

## Delivery and tracking

### How do I track my shipment?

Enter your tracking number on the Northwind website. ⟦Tracking numbers start with NW and the tracking page is updated every 15 minutes.⟧

### What are the standard delivery times?

⟦Standard delivery within the Benelux takes 1 to 2 working days, and within the rest of the EU 3 to 5 working days.⟧

## Claims

### How long do I have to report damage?

⟦Damage must be reported within 7 days of delivery.⟧ Use the claims form and attach photographs of the packaging.

### What is the compensation for lost goods?

⟦Standard liability is EUR 20 per kg unless a higher value was declared before dispatch.⟧ ⟦Customers with a Master Services Agreement are governed by that agreement instead of these standard terms.⟧

## Pallets and packaging

### What is the maximum weight of a pallet?

⟦The maximum weight of a standard pallet is 1,000 kg.⟧

### Do you charge a fuel surcharge?

⟦A fuel surcharge of 6.5% of the freight is applied to all shipments.⟧

## Support

### How can I reach customer support?

⟦Customer support is available Monday to Friday from 07:00 to 19:00 CET on +31 10 555 0100.⟧ Outside these hours you can use the contact form.
