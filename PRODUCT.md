# Scribe product

Scribe is a calm knowledge workspace for people answering questions from contracts, policies, research, and operational documents. Its promise: add sources, ask a question, and inspect the evidence behind every answer.

## Principles

1. Evidence is part of the answer, not an afterthought.
2. Source state is always legible: queued, processing, ready, failed, retrying, or deleted.
3. Missing evidence is stated plainly; Scribe never implies certainty it does not have.
4. The workspace optimizes three tasks: add sources, ask questions, inspect evidence.
5. Retrieval diagnostics are opt-in. Commercial surfaces are feature flagged.
6. Core workflows meet WCAG 2.2 AA, including keyboard use, 200% zoom, reduced motion, and 44px touch targets.

## Portfolio success

A first-time visitor can understand Scribe, add a document, ask a question, open a citation at its supporting passage, and recognize unsupported answers without instruction.
